Buddyverse Source on GitHub

Beta 0.1.0 · early beta

Your own AI buddies, on your own PC

Buddyverse is a Windows app, an Android app and a web app for iPhone. It runs Hermes Agent on your PC with your own AI model account, and shows each of your bots as an animated buddy. Your phone connects only to your PC. There is no shared server.

You need: a Windows 10/11 PC, an AI model account or API key. Optional: an Android phone or an iPhone (iOS 18.2+).

iPhone / browser

The link comes from your PC

No download. After setup, the PC app shows a link to the web version your own PC serves. Open it in Safari, then Share → Add to Home Screen.

Setup in 4 steps

  1. Install. Run Buddyverse-Setup.exe. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway (the installer isn't code-signed yet).
  2. Choose your login. Open Buddyverse. Pick a username and password. Setup then installs Hermes Agent (its official installer; the beta uses Hermes' canary build), the phone link and an empty notes vault. If a step fails, press Retry.
  3. Connect your AI model. Choose a provider, paste an API key or sign in, and pick a model. Your keys stay on your PC.
  4. Connect your phone. Android: install the app, then scan the pairing QR code on your PC and sign in. iPhone: open the web link in Safari and add it to the Home Screen.

The phone link changes when your PC restarts: open "Phone" in the PC app and scan again. Nothing starts by itself when you sign in to Windows.

Let an AI agent do it

Use Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI or Hermes? Paste our ready-made setup prompt. It asks before installing anything and never asks for your passwords or keys in the chat.

Privacy

Everything runs on your PC: the agents, your notes, your login and your keys. Your phone connects only to your PC, through a Cloudflare link your PC opens. This page has no cookies, no trackers, no analytics and no external fonts.
